# Opportunity score

Weekly-winner weighting: pain 25%, willingness to pay 25%, evidence quality 20%, distribution fit 15%, CTA testability 15%. Each dimension is scored 0–5; weighted total is the weighted score divided by 5 and expressed out of 100.

| Dimension | Score | Weight | Evidence and uncertainty |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Pain | 4/5 | 25% | Founders report competitors being recommended and high tool costs. Lost pipeline is plausible, but most quotes do not quantify loss. |
| Willingness to pay | 3/5 | 25% | A crowded $29–$149 self-serve market and $295+ tools prove category spend. They do not prove demand for a page-repair report. |
| Evidence quality | 4/5 | 20% | Search/CPC, competitor prices, citation studies, videos, and user quotes triangulate the market. Some are secondary and all require freshness checks. |
| Distribution fit | 3/5 | 15% | Buyers gather in reachable SEO/SaaS communities, but success demands an intense founder-led content and outreach grind. No owned audience is assumed. |
| CTA testability | 5/5 | 15% | A one-URL audit has a crisp input and observable completion, paid-report upsell, page edit, and timed citation rerun. |

**Weighted total: 75/100.** Calculation: `(4×25 + 3×25 + 4×20 + 3×15 + 5×15) ÷ 5 = 75`.

Confidence is medium. Biggest unknowns: whether founders trust deterministic recommendations, whether observed engine variation overwhelms the signal, and whether the $499 service converts.
