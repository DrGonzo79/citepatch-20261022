# Office Hours: product diagnostic

## Specific customer

Start with founder-led B2B SaaS companies at $20K–$200K MRR that already maintain public pricing and documentation, can name 3–5 competitors, and personally run growth. Exclude enterprises, local businesses, and founders who cannot edit their pages.

## Painful status quo

The founder discovers manually that ChatGPT recommends a rival. Low-cost trackers show mention share; enterprise suites expose more data but cost hundreds per month. Neither closes the loop from **lost prompt → winning competitor URL → exact page edit → measured rerun** at a founder-friendly price.

## Desperate use case

“A buyer asks an AI assistant which tool to choose, my rival’s pricing page is cited, and I have a launch next week. Tell me what to change on my pricing page today and prove whether it helped.” Urgency comes from a revenue page and an observable competitor win—not general brand monitoring.

## Narrowest paid wedge

Sell a human-reviewed, 48-hour pricing-page citation repair report for $499 before building a recurring tracker. Input: one pricing URL, three competitors, 25 buying prompts, four engines. Output: evidence snapshots, missing facts, six edits, and a rerun schedule. The $49 software tier comes only after at least ten customers buy the report and five ask for ongoing reruns.

## Observation and validation

1. Recruit 20 founder-led SaaS companies from AEO/SaaS communities; screen for an editable public pricing page and a real competitor citation loss.
2. Manually deliver five free tear-downs. Observe whether the founder can execute the edit without a call.
3. Offer the next ten at $499. Success is three paid reports and at least two shipped edits—not “interest.”
4. Rerun the exact prompt set at days 7, 14, and 28. Report variance and avoid causal claims without a stable baseline.
5. Ask paid customers to pre-commit to $49/month monitoring. Continue only if at least five accept.

## Future fit

If the service works, the software becomes an evidence ledger for page-level citation experiments. The durable asset is a structured corpus of page changes, engine observations, vertical context, and outcomes. Generative rewriting stays subordinate to an explainable rubric and human approval.

## Verdict

Proceed as a service-first validation, not a tracker-first build. The report’s demand evidence is good enough to run paid tests, but category crowding and citation randomness make another dashboard a weak bet.
